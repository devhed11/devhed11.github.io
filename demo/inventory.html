📐 How This Is Calculated
Days on Hand
Current warehouse quantity ÷ average daily usage over the selected window. Answers: "If we stop ordering today, how long until we run out?"
Days on Hand = Warehouse Qty ÷ (Avg Monthly Usage ÷ 30)
Receive-Based Metrics
Last Received and Orders YTD come directly from receive transactions logged in this system. Avg Days Between Orders requires 2 or more receive entries for the same product — it will show "—" until that history exists.
⚠️ Note on "Annual Turns": The classic formula is COGS ÷ Average Inventory — not current stock. Using today's warehouse snapshot inflates turns right before a restock and deflates them right after. This metric is intentionally omitted until average inventory data accumulates from receive history. Days on Hand is the more actionable number.
Color guide: ● Critical < 14 days  ·  ● Order Soon 14–30 days  ·  ● Well Stocked > 30 days  ·  ● No Usage Data (not actively used)

📐 How Efficiency Scores Work
Each job is compared to the team average for that specific service type. A score of 75 = you're exactly average. Higher = you used fewer chemicals than average for those job types. Lower = higher-than-average chemical spend. Grades: A ≥ 90 · B ≥ 75 · C ≥ 60 · D ≥ 45 · F < 45
